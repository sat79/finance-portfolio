## Why the first runs failed
The initial loader rejected shifted candle openings in February 2018, for example 09:58:14.789 UTC. No strategy performance was calculated in those failed runs. The correction excludes those candles and blocks their overlapping regular intervals; it never rounds candle times or invents replacement prices. The resulting complete 2018-2026 grids each contain 1,847 missing/blocked bars. Individual positions that cross unavailable intervals are flagged and their results remain provisional. The rolling evaluation reported no gap-affected positions.

## Rolling combined account
A single 10,000 USDT starting account, shared across BTC and ETH. Each six-month fold selects an exit within each asset/family using only its preceding 24 months' net return. Families without a positive training result, or selected training variants affected by gaps, remain inactive. The same selections are used for the doubled-cost scenario. Positions close at fold boundaries. Multiple early folds remain entirely in cash.

## Interpretation and next research direction
- The five- and fifteen-minute pullback variants lose substantially after costs on both assets. Do not deploy them or infer that adding indicators will repair them.
- Hourly pullbacks are closer to break-even but still lose in the complete normal-cost screen.
- Four-hour divergence shows small positive historical returns with only 21-25 positions per asset/exit variant. That is insufficient evidence of a dependable edge.
- BTC daily divergence has 57.1% winners from only seven positions. It does not reach 60%, and the tiny sample cannot support confidence in that rate.
- The daily SMA200 rebound/partial-exit module is unsuccessful in this implementation.
- Combining selected modules has not made the system economically persuasive: normal-cost profit factor is only about 1.03, with negative cost-stress expectancy.

Retain the failed tests. Further work should change the economic hypothesis, such as distinguishing established trends from volatility expansions, rather than tune the same evaluation history until a desired percentage appears. Any new family needs a dated experiment specification and genuinely later forward observations.

## Limits
The tests cover core accounting and timing failure modes, not every possible market condition. The universe contains two surviving assets. Five-minute OHLC cannot resolve every intrabar path or order-book fill. Gap-affected screen rows are provisional. Lot sizes/minimum notional are fixed research assumptions. Returns use capped exposure and should not be compared with fully invested buy-and-hold without considering that difference; the CSV also includes an exposure-scaled buy-and-hold reference.

Wilson intervals do not adjust for trade dependence. Time-block bootstrap intervals, reconstructed historical exchange filters, worst-month/year and time-underwater summaries are not yet implemented. Combined drawdown above uses daily equity and can understate intraday drawdown. No 60% win-rate claim, live order execution or Meta Signals replication is established.
