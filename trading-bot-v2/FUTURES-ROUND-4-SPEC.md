## Eight configurations, two declared research groups
Each group has 1h and 4h signal timeframes; entries execute at the next five-minute opening after the signal candle closes.

FIB transfer (four): retain round-3 confirmed Fibonacci + hidden RSI divergence entries in matching daily trends, and lower-band/RSI rejection in sideways conditions. Mirror price geometry for shorts, using signed OHLC only to calculate signals. Genuine futures signed arithmetic handles account P&L. Fixed 2R target versus adaptive 3R cap with cost-adjusted breakeven at 1R and completed-bar 2-ATR trailing after 2R. Adaptive range entries also exit after a close reaches SMA20. Initial stop: more distant of 1.5 ATR and the last five lows minus 0.25 ATR (mirror highs for shorts).

BREAK refinement (four): close above prior 20-bar high, previous close not already above its corresponding breakout level, close above EMA21, EMA21 above EMA55, matching daily uptrend. Mirror for shorts/downtrend. Fixed 2R versus 3R targets. Initial stop: more distant of 2 ATR and last five lows minus 0.25 ATR, mirrored for shorts. Also exit at the next opening after a close crosses back through EMA21. No range entry module.

All configurations exit after daily regime changes away from entry regime, or after 5 days for 1h / 20 days for 4h. Daily regimes retain SMA200, its 20-day change normalized by simple ATR14, and Wilder ADX14: up/down require ADX >=20 and slope beyond +/-0.5 ATR; sideways ADX <20; otherwise transition. Use only a daily candle closing strictly before the signal candle closes. Unknown and transition prohibit new entries. No future pivots or unfinished candle information.

## Account, costs and leverage
Initial equity 10,000 USDT; one directional position; planned risk 0.5% of current equity including assumed entry and stop-exit costs. Entry notional limited to the chosen 1x/2x/3x/5x ceiling after entry fees. No ceiling exceeds 5x. Reserve sufficient entry equity for notional/ceiling plus fees. Stop-distance entry filter: at least 3 times estimated round-trip fees/slippage. Fixed research quantity step 0.001 BTC, minimum notional 10 USDT; these are not historical exchange filter claims.
Normal fee 0.05% each side and adverse slippage 0.02% each side, fixed assumptions. Zero/double-cost replays affect fees/slippage, not historical funding rates. Futures wallet changes by realized signed P&L, fees and funding; notional is not subtracted as if buying spot.
Use contract prices for fills, mark prices for unrealized equity and margin. Cross-equity maintenance stress assumes 1% current notional plus a 0.5% liquidation-fee reserve, with 2% maintenance sensitivity. Liquidation charges the additional 0.5% assumed fee. These are research stresses, not an exchange-exact historical liquidation model. If liquidation and a stop are both possible within a candle, resolve liquidation first conservatively. Opening exits precede subsequent intrabar events. Stop/target ambiguity resolves stop first. No favorable target-gap improvement. Missing final exit prices cause failure rather than imputation.
Observe account leverage at five-minute opens and close the entire position if above the user's 5x maximum. Prices can raise exposure beyond the ceiling between observations; report observed breaches/forced exits rather than promising continuous enforcement from candles. Lower leverage ceilings limit entry sizing; the hard ongoing account cap is 5x. No averaging down, hedging, martingale or automatic live trading.

## Funding chronology
Preserve raw event timestamps. Exact five-minute-boundary funding belongs to a carried position before orders at that opening. Intrabar events prohibit new entries for that bar. For carried positions, debit adverse intrabar funding even when an exit could precede settlement; award credits only if the position survives that bar. For uncertain intrabar mark values, use the higher mark for debits and lower mark for credits. Flag ambiguous settlements. This is a conservative candle-resolution approximation, not tick-accurate event execution. Missing mark/contract bars flag positions; no missing funding is silently treated as zero.

## Comparisons and selection
For every configuration run: long-only, short-only and combined at normal costs/5x; combined at zero/double costs/5x; combined at normal costs with 1x/2x/3x ceilings; and combined with 2% maintenance/5x. This totals 72 screen rows; cost and cap sensitivity rows are not separate entry-rule optimizations.
Rolling: preceding 24 calendar months choose among the eight combined configurations at normal costs/5x, requiring at least 20 closed positions, positive mean net R, positive return, profit factor >1.1, no gap-affected positions and no liquidation. Choose highest net return; tie by name. Hold for six months, or cash if none qualify. Repeat chosen configuration with normal and double costs without reselection. Force liquidation at fold boundaries. First fold January 2022, final partial fold July–August 2026.
