# Direction-selection refinement, after the first futures run
15 September 2026, recorded before executing this additional comparison.

Observed round-4 result: combined rolling selection lost 5.14% at normal costs and 7.38% at doubled costs. Directional screens show asymmetric results, particularly weak Fibonacci shorts. Hypothesis: forcing both directions for a chosen configuration may reduce performance; prior training data should be allowed to select one direction.

Keep all eight entry/exit configurations, costs, risk, 5x maximum, dates, 24-month training and six-month execution unchanged. Expand each training comparison to long-only, short-only and combined, 24 combinations. Choose ONE configuration/direction with at least 20 positions, positive mean net R and return, profit factor >1.1, no gap-affected trades and no liquidation. Rank by net return, then configuration name, then direction. None eligible means cash. Same choice at doubled costs, no extra positions or hedging. No full-period directional winner is hard-coded.

This is another research trial after reviewing the first futures results, on already exposed history. Report it alongside the failed original selection, preserve every training candidate, and do not label improvement as independent confirmation. No further parameter search is planned in this bounded run.
