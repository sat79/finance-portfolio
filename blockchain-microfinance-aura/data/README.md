# Dissertation-derived inputs
These small CSVs transcribe selected numeric cells from the supplied final dissertation, not from “Untitled document”. Original documents remain unchanged and are not distributed.

- `aura_scores.csv`: Table 5, three contexts and four scores out of 25, plus the rounded reported total.
- `bass_parameters.csv`: Table 3, innovation p, imitation q and market ceiling m ranges for three regions.
- These are dissertation assumptions or illustrative scores. Their real-world calibration, underlying participant measurements and source-level scoring evidence were not supplied. They must not be described as verified company performance.
- The new audit assumes annual Bass parameters because the surrounding narrative discusses years; the original table does not explicitly specify time units.
- Dirichlet(1,1,1,1) weights, seed 79 and 10,000 preference draws are NEW audit choices. They measure sensitivity to a chosen preference distribution, not the probability a service is good or that a framework is valid.
- The separate fee arithmetic check uses the final text's 12.3% baseline and 4.2–8.7% alternative range.
