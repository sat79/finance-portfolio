## What remains weak

The evidence and schema do not support an independently verified causal bridge. Quarterly figures remain quoted disclosure evidence, not normalized quantitative claim records. The sample does not quantify contribution, comparable-scope growth, credit costs or capital headroom. The annual numeric records retain long decimal strings because the schema requires exact copying; this is auditable but less readable. The unused dividend proposal is an analytical omission, not a validator error. Its approval/payment status cannot be inferred from the proposal.

The validator establishes exact reference/quote/record matching. A copied but semantically unrelated valid excerpt could still pass; human relevance review remains necessary. It does not evaluate materiality, causation or all natural-language numerical claims. No claim of automated semantic verification is made.
